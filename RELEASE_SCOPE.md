# Release scope

## Included for first submission

1. Three finalized AMYCLIP checkpoints with SHA-256 verification.
2. Small sequence and frozen-embedding samples for execution checks.
3. The final model architecture and dual-endpoint loss implementation.
4. A compact training-code execution example.
5. One inference input and its result generated from the released checkpoints.

## Deferred unless requested during revision

1. Complete training, development, and evaluation datasets.
2. Data acquisition, curation, clustering, and split-construction programs.
3. Full inference, sequence-search, generation, and candidate-triage workflows.
4. Exploratory analyses and manuscript-production files.

The deferred material is not required to inspect the finalized architecture,
verify checkpoint integrity, execute the reported objective, or reproduce the
included inference example.
